# Controllable and Understandable Learning: Field-by-Field Evidence Map

This map investigates how to build models whose behavior an engineer can understand, deliberately change, and evaluate against an intended purpose. It covers all 59 entries in the preceding atlas, adds 18 construction-oriented entries, and extends explicitly to LLMs, vision-language models, vision-language-action models, and world models. Literature was retrieved on October 6, 2026; this is complete coverage of that declared inventory, not a claim to have exhausted these literatures.

The scope is forward design, learning, system identification, exposed model interfaces, behavioral experiments, and mathematical assurance. It excludes LLM reverse engineering, circuit discovery, activation patching, and circuit-level mechanistic interpretability. Developmental work is included only for statistical learning dynamics and possible training interventions.

## The answer in brief

The strongest constructive examples do not “understand everything” about a network. They choose a property that matters, encode it in a model or learning procedure, and establish what follows: specified symmetries, monotonic input-output relations, contraction, energy balance, explicit concept interfaces, or checked symbolic deductions ([You et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.06680), [Revay, Wang, and Manchester](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.05942), [Kondor](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12541325/), [Koh et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/koh20a/koh20a.pdf), [Trinh et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06747-5)). These are different kinds of understanding and control; none of those results establishes general semantic alignment of a capable model.

Useful capability can also be built intentionally through task distributions and feedback: compositional meta-learning, verifiable-outcome reinforcement learning, action-conditioned world-model learning, and language-to-action training all have concrete demonstrations ([Lake and Baroni](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37880371/), [Guo et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12443585/), [Hafner et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08744-2), [Brohan et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2307.15818v1)). The research question is whether the desired capability and the desired correction behavior generalize together, not merely whether a model can be made smaller, more stable, or easier to describe.

## What the terms mean here

These are the working definitions used to organize the evidence. They deliberately separate properties that are often conflated.

| Term | Operational question |
|---|---|
| Understandable specification | Can the engineer state what the model should do, what it must not do, and what remains uncertain? |
| Structural interpretability | Can a person inspect the actual rules, equations, modules, or named variables used for the decision? |
| Identifiability | Could the available data distinguish this proposed mechanism or state from an alternative that predicts the same observations? |
| Intervention validity | Does changing a named variable produce the intended behavioral change, without hidden side effects or a coordinate-dependent interpretation? |
| Controllability | Can permitted interventions reach specified outcomes from specified starting conditions, within time and resource limits? |
| Alignment | Does the model pursue the legitimate intended objective, including constraints, uncertainty, and correction, rather than merely a convenient proxy? |
| Assurance | What evidence or theorem supports that claim, for which system, assumptions, distribution, and horizon? |

“Interpretability” below means understanding useful for prediction, correction, and design. A readable explanation is not assumed to be a faithful account of a model’s computation; behavioral experiments have demonstrated misleading chain-of-thought explanations in specific LLM settings ([Turpin, Michael, Perez, and Bowman](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/ed3fea9033a80fea1376299fa7863f4a-Abstract-Conference.html)).

## Reading the field tables

The second column contains established constructions or explicitly scoped research results, with author anchors. The third states the evidence boundary and the missing transfer to intended behavior. The final column is an original proposed experiment or engineering check, not a published result.

The stable IDs make coverage auditable and connect this document to the interactive atlas. “No guarantee established” means none is established by the cited work for that stronger claim; it is not a claim that no relevant paper exists anywhere.

### Statistical learning

| ID / field | Established understanding or design handle | Scope and missing transfer | Proposed decisive test |
|---|---|---|---|
| ridge / Linear and ridge regression | An explicit coefficient map; Gaussian likelihood and prior connect ridge to Bayesian estimation ([Rasmussen and Williams](https://gaussianprocess.org/gpml/chapters/RW2.pdf)). | Inspectable coefficients describe the fitted predictor, not necessarily causal effects; its uncertainty depends on the model assumptions ([GPML](https://gaussianprocess.org/gpml/chapters/RW2.pdf)). | Intervene on actual inputs, break correlations, and check whether coefficient-based predictions remain valid. Compare to unregularized and sparse linear models. |
| svm / SVMs and kernels | The kernel specifies similarity and the margin specifies a classification objective; this makes the hypothesis class and decision rule explicit ([Cortes and Vapnik](https://research.google/pubs/support-vector-networks/), [Rasmussen and Williams](https://gaussianprocess.org/gpml/chapters/RW6.pdf)). | A margin in feature space is not calibrated probability or a causal state variable; the kernel defines what differences count as small ([GPML](https://gaussianprocess.org/gpml/chapters/RW6.pdf)). | Test perturbations in physical/task coordinates, not only kernel distance; compare an explicit feature model with the nonlinear kernel. |
| gp / Gaussian processes | Posterior means and covariance expose a model of uncertainty; GP-based dynamics can support conditional safe-learning certificates ([Rasmussen and Williams](https://gaussianprocess.org/gpml/chapters/RW2.pdf), [Berkenkamp, Turchetta, Schoellig, and Krause](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1705.08551)). | The safe-learning theorem needs uniform confidence coverage and regularity, not merely a plotted posterior interval; the experiment relaxes parts of the theoretical setup ([Berkenkamp et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1705.08551)). | Test simultaneous coverage across adaptively visited states and challenge the kernel with an omitted dynamics regime. |
| kmeans / k-means and quantization | Centroids and assignments give an explicit partition minimizing within-cluster squared distances ([Lloyd, MacQueen, Hartigan, and Wong lineage](https://web.mit.edu/r/current/lib/R/library/stats/html/kmeans.html)). | A geometric partition has no action-conditioned transition model; distance-based clustering alone does not supply a control state ([R documentation](https://web.mit.edu/r/current/lib/R/library/stats/html/kmeans.html)). | Within each cluster, apply the same actions and test whether rewards, constraints, and future observations agree. Split clusters when they do not. |
| trees / Trees and ensembles | A small tree exposes decision predicates; ensembles are useful predictive baselines but need not remain easy to inspect ([Hastie, Tibshirani, and Friedman](https://hastie.su.domains/ElemStatLearn/main.html)). | Good tabular prediction does not establish causal or temporal correctness; favorable tree benchmarks concern specified datasets, not universal superiority ([Grinsztajn, Oyallon, and Varoquaux](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.08815)). | Compare a compact tree, boosted ensemble, and neural predictor on intervention response and shifted operating regimes, not just random-split accuracy. |
| latent / Mixtures, HMMs, and latent-variable models | Explicit emission and transition models make probabilistic assumptions inspectable; filtering computes beliefs about unobserved state ([Särkkä and Svensson](https://users.aalto.fi/~ssarkka/pub/bfs_book_2023_online.pdf)). | Latent representations can be non-identifiable; observations alone need not choose the intended semantic factors ([Locatello et al.](https://www.arxiv.org/pdf/2007.14184)). | Construct observationally equivalent alternatives and seek an action, sensor, or intervention that distinguishes them. |

### Signals, images, and estimation

| ID / field | Established understanding or design handle | Scope and missing transfer | Proposed decisive test |
|---|---|---|---|
| conv / Convolution, FIR, and IIR filters | Impulse responses and frequency responses specify signal transformations; LTI state-space layers also admit convolutional evaluation ([Oppenheim and Schafer teaching lineage](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-6-dtsp-discrete-time-signal-processing/pages/signal-processing-courses/), [Gu, Goel, and Ré](https://arxiv.org/html/2111.00396v3)). | Filter stability or attenuation says what happens to signals, not whether a task-relevant event was preserved ([MIT DSP](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-6-dtsp-discrete-time-signal-processing/pages/signal-processing-courses/)). | Inject known signals and hazards across frequencies; measure attenuation, delay, and missed-event cost. |
| fourier / Fourier and spectral analysis | Explicit spectral representations support filter and feedback-loop analysis ([Oppenheim and Schafer](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-6-dtsp-discrete-time-signal-processing/pages/signal-processing-courses/), [Åström and Murray](https://people.duke.edu/~hpgavin/SystemID/References/Astrom-Feedback-2006.pdf)). | Spectral information is a mathematical description, not a semantic decomposition; feedback stability depends on the complete loop ([Åström and Murray](https://people.duke.edu/~hpgavin/SystemID/References/Astrom-Feedback-2006.pdf)). | Measure phase and gain under actual sample rates, delays, and actuator saturation, then compare with the predicted loop behavior. |
| wavelet / Wavelets and multiresolution | Scale-specific filtering is explicit; scattering networks add prescribed wavelet/modulus operations with nonexpansiveness and deformation bounds under stated conditions ([Mallat](https://www.di.ens.fr/~mallat/papiers/MallatTheory89.pdf), [Bruna and Mallat](https://www.di.ens.fr/~mallat/papiers/Bruna-Mallat-Pami-Scat.pdf)). | Representation stability is not classification correctness, and finite averaging deliberately discards information ([Bruna and Mallat](https://www.di.ens.fr/~mallat/papiers/Bruna-Mallat-Pami-Scat.pdf)). | Vary nuisance deformations and task-critical fine structure separately; test whether the chosen invariant removes information needed for control. |
| wiener / Wiener filtering | A stochastic linear-estimation problem can be solved using signal and noise statistics ([Wiener](https://direct.mit.edu/books/oa-monograph/4361/chapter/187129/Introduction), [Kalman](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~motionplanning/papers/sbp_papers/k/Kalman1960.pdf)). | Optimality is relative to the estimator class and statistical assumptions, not arbitrary nonlinear or nonstationary environments ([Kalman](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~motionplanning/papers/sbp_papers/k/Kalman1960.pdf)). | Shift noise spectra and event rates; compare causal filtering, delayed smoothing, and downstream control error. |
| kalman / Kalman and Kalman–Bucy | Explicit states, measurement maps, innovations, and covariance updates expose the estimator; linear-Gaussian filtering is exact ([Kalman](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~motionplanning/papers/sbp_papers/k/Kalman1960.pdf), [Kalman and Bucy](https://people.duke.edu/~hpgavin/SystemID/References/KalmanBucy-ASME-JBE-1961.pdf)). | State estimation is not actuation; uncertainty and observer performance depend on observability and the dynamics/noise model ([Särkkä and Svensson](https://users.aalto.fi/~ssarkka/pub/bfs_book_2023_online.pdf)). | Check innovation consistency, sensor bias, unobservable modes, and actuator loss before claiming successful feedback control. |
| nonlinearfilter / EKF, UKF, cubature, particles, smoothing | Nonlinear Bayesian estimation exposes a belief rather than pretending one point state is certain; different approximations handle different posterior shapes ([Särkkä and Svensson](https://users.aalto.fi/~ssarkka/pub/bfs_book_2023_online.pdf)). | Approximation error, particle degeneracy, and model mismatch matter; smoothing uses future data unavailable to an online controller ([Särkkä and Svensson](https://users.aalto.fi/~ssarkka/pub/bfs_book_2023_online.pdf)). | Compare posterior coverage and failure detection with an exact small-system reference; evaluate online and hindsight estimates separately. |
| bilateral / Bilateral image filtering | Spatial and intensity similarity explicitly govern edge-preserving averaging ([Tomasi and Manduchi](https://www.cs.jhu.edu/~misha/ReadingSeminar/Papers/Tomasi98.pdf)). | Preserving image edges is not preserving objects, small hazards, or physically meaningful boundaries ([Tomasi and Manduchi](https://www.cs.jhu.edu/~misha/ReadingSeminar/Papers/Tomasi98.pdf)). | Change contrast, lighting, and sensor noise while holding geometry fixed; measure the effect on estimated clearance and object identity. |
| anisotropic / Anisotropic diffusion | An explicit PDE and diffusivity function regulate spatial smoothing ([Perona and Malik](https://openreview.net/forum?id=R2gc38YmhX)). | Image-evolution time is not physical time; edge-sensitive smoothing does not establish a correct scene model ([Perona and Malik](https://openreview.net/forum?id=R2gc38YmhX)). | Audit the numerical discretization and test whether small task-critical boundaries survive the full perception-to-action pipeline. |

### Neural architectures

| ID / field | Established understanding or design handle | Scope and missing transfer | Proposed decisive test |
|---|---|---|---|
| cnn / CNNs | Local weight sharing gives a designed spatial structure; fixed scattering provides a related construction with stronger specified signal guarantees ([LeCun, Bottou, Bengio, and Haffner](https://leon.bottou.org/papers/lecun-98h), [Bruna and Mallat](https://www.di.ens.fr/~mallat/papiers/Bruna-Mallat-Pami-Scat.pdf)). | A learned filter stack does not inherit every scattering guarantee; structural locality is not object-level meaning ([Bruna and Mallat](https://www.di.ens.fr/~mallat/papiers/Bruna-Mallat-Pami-Scat.pdf)). | Train matched CNN, scattering-plus-readout, and equivariant models; separate geometric robustness from concept and intervention accuracy. |
| rnn / RNNs | The recurrence is a learned dynamical system; contraction constraints bound sensitivity and imply fading dependence on initial state ([Elman](https://pages.ucsd.edu/~rbelew/courses/readings/elman90-fsit.pdf), [Miller and Hardt](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.10369)). | A stable recurrence can forget information that the task needs; hidden state is not automatically an identified world state ([Miller and Hardt](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.10369)). | Combine long-delay memory tasks with perturbation recovery, and measure whether a correction survives without erasing unrelated knowledge. |
| lstm / LSTM and GRU | Gates create adjustable memory paths; sufficient contraction conditions have been studied for LSTMs ([Hochreiter and Schmidhuber](https://mlanthology.org/neco/1997/hochreiter1997neco-long/), [Cho et al.](https://www.arxiv.org/abs/1406.1078), [Miller and Hardt](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.10369)). | The cited LSTM conditions are restrictive and do not constitute a universal theorem for all LSTMs or GRUs; gates are not inherently interpretable Bayesian gains ([Miller and Hardt](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.10369)). | Test gate-constrained and unconstrained versions on retained instructions, noise rejection, and contradictory late evidence at matched compute. |
| attention / Transformers and attention | Attention, context construction, objectives, and interfaces can be designed explicitly; behavior can be shaped through instruction training ([Vaswani et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762v5), [Ouyang et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02155)). | Architecture specification does not identify semantic variables; instruction-following improvements are empirical, not all-input compliance guarantees ([Ouyang et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02155)). | Train on counterfactual instruction conflicts and held-out compositions; assess capability and correction retention without inspecting circuits. |
| linearattention / Linear attention | Feature-map attention permits recurrent sufficient accumulations for the chosen attention formula ([Katharopoulos, Vyas, Pappas, and Fleuret](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/katharopoulos20a.html)). | An efficient recurrence is not proof that its compressed state preserves every future task-relevant distinction ([Katharopoulos et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/katharopoulos20a.html)). | Increase distractors and delayed queries while requiring exact state tracking; compare memory errors and recovery after explicit correction. |
| s4 / Structured SSMs and S4 | State-transition structure and discretization provide deliberate memory timescales and equivalent recurrence/convolution views ([Gu, Goel, and Ré](https://arxiv.org/html/2111.00396v3)). | A structured linear layer is not a physically identified latent model, and a nonlinear stack needs its own analysis ([Gu et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2111.00396v3)). | Vary sampling intervals and task timescales; test both memory retention and identified input-output response of the whole model. |
| mamba / Selective SSMs | Input-dependent updates give learned retention and selection rather than one fixed filter ([Gu and Dao](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.00752v2)). | Input-dependent gating does not establish semantic editability; expressivity and trainability of state tracking need separate evidence ([Terzić et al.](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/77b830c18836a9b2e1395a4936dd687a-Paper-Conference.pdf)). | Test held-out action sequences, length extrapolation, and persistent rule changes against transformer, LSTM, and symbolic-state baselines. |
| ssd / Mamba-2 and state-space duality | Semiseparable structure gives a precise connection between SSM and attention variants ([Dao and Gu](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.21060)). | An algebraic duality is not an alignment theorem or a statement that all attention architectures have identical behavior ([Dao and Gu](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.21060)). | Hold the mathematical operator fixed across implementations, then separate numerical differences from learned behavioral differences. |
| hyena / Gated long convolution | Long convolutions and gates provide an explicit sequence-operator design ([Poli et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/poli23a/poli23a.pdf)). | Faster sequence processing or competitive loss does not show semantic controllability or accurate state transitions ([Poli et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/poli23a/poli23a.pdf)). | Compare at matched training and inference budgets on compositional state tracking, exact retrieval, and correction persistence. |
| hybrid / SSM-attention hybrids | Recurrence and explicit attention provide different memory mechanisms that can be combined ([De, Smith, and collaborators, Griffin](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.19427)). | A hybrid inherits neither perfect recall nor a general control guarantee; Griffin documents retrieval-related limitations ([Griffin](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.19427)). | Ablate recurrence, attention, and explicit memory while keeping data and evaluation fixed; look for complementary gains rather than added parameters alone. |
| cde / Neural CDEs | A continuous path drives hidden-state evolution, suitable for irregular time-series modeling ([Kidger, Morrill, Foster, and Lyons](https://www.maths.ox.ac.uk/node/36672)). | “Controlled” means driven by a path in this mathematical construction; it does not itself guarantee an engineer can steer outcomes ([Kidger et al.](https://www.maths.ox.ac.uk/node/36672)). | Change interpolation, sample timing, and missingness independently of the physical process; require stable decision quality and justified uncertainty. |
| graph / Graph networks | Entities and relations are explicit architectural choices, enabling relational inductive biases ([Battaglia et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.01261)). | A graph can encode the wrong entities or interactions; relational structure alone does not establish causal or physical truth ([Battaglia et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.01261)). | Hold out numbers and arrangements of objects, intervene on edges, and test predicted action consequences under new compositions. |

### Generative and physical models

| ID / field | Established understanding or design handle | Scope and missing transfer | Proposed decisive test |
|---|---|---|---|
| node / Neural ODEs | An explicit learned vector field supports continuous-time modeling; the solver is part of the computation ([Chen, Rubanova, Bettencourt, and Duvenaud](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.07366)). | A vector field is not automatically stable, identifiable, or physically meaningful; numerical integration adds a separate error source ([Chen et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.07366)). | Compare unconstrained, contracting, and physics-structured fields under changed step size and unseen controls; measure real rollout error. |
| diffusion / Generative diffusion | A known corruption process and learned denoiser define generation; classifier-free guidance creates an explicit conditioning strength ([Ho, Jain, and Abbeel](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.11239), [Ho and Salimans](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.12598)). | Guidance trades fidelity and diversity; it is not a guarantee that every requested property holds ([Ho and Salimans](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.12598)). | Measure reachable combinations of attributes, per-attribute calibration, and diversity loss as conditioning strength changes. |
| score / Score SDEs and probability-flow ODEs | Scores define stochastic or deterministic distribution transport; exact-score constructions can share time marginals ([Song et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.13456)). | Equal marginals are not equal paths; approximate inverse-problem sampling is not exact posterior or strict measurement consistency ([Song et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.13456), [Chung et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.14687)). | On a tractable inverse problem, compare posterior coverage, measurement residual, and path constraints against an exact reference. |
| flow / Flow matching | Train a vector field on specified probability paths; the same objective can generate action chunks ([Lipman et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.02747), [Black et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.24164)). | Matching an action distribution does not certify the safety of the executed trajectory; generation time and environment time differ ([Black et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.24164)). | Change integrator steps and execution chunk length separately; test both deadline misses and environment-state violations. |
| bridge / Schrödinger bridges | Path-space transport makes a distribution-steering objective explicit ([De Bortoli, Thornton, Heng, and Doucet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.01357)). | Reaching endpoint distributions is different from remaining in a safe set at every physical instant ([De Bortoli et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.01357), [Ames et al.](http://ames.caltech.edu/ames2019control.pdf)). | Add path constraints, then test whether endpoint success hides intermediate violations and how approximated dynamics change the result. |
| dit / Diffusion transformers | Separate the transformer backbone from the denoising objective and sampling procedure ([Peebles and Xie](https://www.wpeebles.com/DiT.html)). | Backbone scaling and generation quality do not imply stronger conditional compliance or intelligible semantic controls ([Peebles and Xie](https://www.wpeebles.com/DiT.html)). | Match data, compute, conditioning interface, and sampler across DiT and U-Net; evaluate joint constraint adherence, not only FID. |
| sindy / SINDy and SINDYc | Sparse equations expose candidate mechanisms, including control inputs; ensembles estimate selection variability ([Brunton, Proctor, and Kutz](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.06682), [Fasel et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.10992)). | Recovery depends on the library, coordinates, excitation, and noise; sparse readable equations can still be the wrong mechanism ([Brunton et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.06682), [Fasel et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.10992)). | Discover dynamics on unseen systems, intervene beyond passive trajectories, and compare control utility with ridge, GP, and neural residual models. |
| koopman / Koopman embeddings | Learn coordinates with approximately linear evolution; stable matrix parameterizations and conditional contraction links are available ([Lusch, Kutz, and Brunton](https://www.lri.fr/~gcharpia/deeppractice/2022/chap_5_biblio/Koopman/KoopmanAE.pdf), [Fan et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.08153v2)). | Stable latent evolution transfers to the physical system only with the required embedding relation and regularity; finite-data fitting leaves invariance error ([Fan et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.08153v2)). | Audit encoder rank, reconstruction, controlled invariance residuals, and decoded closed-loop behavior on held-out regions. |
| hnn / Hamiltonian and Lagrangian networks | Learn energy or a Lagrangian and derive dynamics from the chosen mechanical structure ([Greydanus, Dzamba, and Yosinski](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.01563), [Cranmer et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.04630)). | Conservative structure can be an incorrect assumption for driven or dissipative systems; a continuous-time identity is not a numerical rollout guarantee ([HNN](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.01563), [LNN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.04630)). | Introduce friction, contact, and actuation; compare conservative, dissipative, and unconstrained models using both energy balance and task success. |
| pinn / Physics-informed networks | PDE residuals supply a scientific learning constraint; solution-error bounds require PDE stability/coercivity and sampling control ([Raissi, Perdikaris, and Karniadakis](https://neuralfields.cs.brown.edu/paper_4.html), [Mishra](https://ethz.ch/content/dam/ethz/special-interest/math/applied-mathematics/camlab-dam/documents/AISE2024/AISE24%2008%2009%20Physics-Informed%20Neural%20Networks%20%E2%80%93%20Theory%20Part%201%20and%202.pdf)). | Small sampled residual is not automatically small solution error; difficult optimization and failure regimes are documented ([Krishnapriyan et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.01050)). | Compare with a converged numerical solver; check boundary conditions, unsampled residuals, conditioning, and downstream control sensitivity. |
| operator / FNO and DeepONet | Learn a function-to-function map across a problem family; DeepONet theory separates encoding, approximation, and reconstruction error ([Li et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.08895), [Lanthaler, Mishra, and Karniadakis](https://academic.oup.com/imatrm/article/6/1/tnac001/6542709?searchresult=1)). | Average operator approximation bounds are not pointwise closed-loop guarantees; optimization, shift, and uncertain data are not all covered by the cited analysis ([Lanthaler et al.](https://academic.oup.com/imatrm/article/6/1/tnac001/6542709?searchresult=1)). | Hold out boundary conditions and geometries; convert validated approximation error into the actual controller’s robustness margin. |

### Action learning and feedback

| ID / field | Established understanding or design handle | Scope and missing transfer | Proposed decisive test |
|---|---|---|---|
| control / Feedback, optimal, robust, and adaptive control | Explicit states, observations, actuators, costs, and disturbance models support observability, reachability, stability, and robustness analysis ([Åström and Murray](https://people.duke.edu/~hpgavin/SystemID/References/Astrom-Feedback-2006.pdf)). | PID/LQR/robust or adaptive designs are conditional on the plant and operating assumptions; feedback cannot repair an undefined objective or nonexistent actuator ([Åström and Murray](https://people.duke.edu/~hpgavin/SystemID/References/Astrom-Feedback-2006.pdf)). | Specify the full loop, then challenge model error, delays, saturation, and sensor loss. Compare learned control against a tuned classical controller. |
| rl / Reinforcement learning | Rewards and transitions define the decision problem; control-as-inference exposes connections to probabilistic learning ([Sutton and Barto](https://web.stanford.edu/class/psych209/Readings/SuttonBartoIPRLBook2ndEd.pdf), [Levine](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.00909v3)). | Optimizing the specified reward does not identify the intended objective; different inference formulations can even distort disturbance treatment ([Levine](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.00909v3)). | Separate reward, task outcome, and constraint measures; test a policy on adversarially selected opportunities to exploit its proxy. |
| world / Model-based RL and world models | Action-conditioned latent dynamics can support imagined policy learning; Dreamer separates world model, actor, and critic ([Hafner et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08744-2)). | Good imagined return does not prove accurate counterfactual dynamics or grounded latent semantics; the cited results are empirical ([Hafner et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08744-2)). | Evaluate action-conditioned calibration, long-horizon state persistence, planner exploitation of model errors, and actual environment return separately. |
| policy / Diffusion policies | Observation-conditioned action sequences support multimodal imitation and receding-horizon execution ([Chi et al.](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2303.04137)). | Demonstration matching and receding-horizon execution do not certify collision avoidance or correction responsiveness ([Chi et al.](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2303.04137)). | Insert a stop instruction or new obstacle mid-chunk; measure response latency, residual motion, and safe task completion. |
| kalnet / Neural filtering and KalmanNet | Retain a state-space estimator structure while learning part of its update, rather than replacing all estimation structure ([Revach et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.10043)). | Learned gains do not automatically inherit classical Kalman optimality or calibrated uncertainty ([Revach et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.10043)). | Compare analytic, learned-gain, and residual estimators under held-out noise and dynamics changes using coverage and closed-loop outcomes. |
| td / TD learning and Q-learning | Explicit Bellman targets and tabular convergence results clarify what is being learned ([Sutton and Barto](https://web.stanford.edu/class/psych209/Readings/SuttonBartoIPRLBook2ndEd.pdf), [Watkins and Dayan](http://www.gatsby.ucl.ac.uk/~dayan/papers/wd92.html)). | Repeated sampling and step-size conditions matter; tabular convergence does not extend automatically to arbitrary nonlinear off-policy learning ([Sutton and Barto](https://web.stanford.edu/class/psych209/Readings/SuttonBartoIPRLBook2ndEd.pdf)). | Start with an exact solvable reference, then add approximation and off-policy shift one at a time; diagnose value error versus decision error. |
| ppo / Policy gradients, PPO, and SAC | Differentiable policy objectives, surrogates, and entropy regularization supply concrete learning levers ([Schulman et al.](https://www.arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347), [Haarnoja et al.](https://github.com/haarnoja/sac)). | Clipping, KL penalties, and policy entropy are not physical safety constraints or proofs of intended behavior ([PPO](https://www.arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347), [Achiam et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/achiam17a/achiam17a.pdf)). | Compare policy updates using actual constraint trajectories and rare failures, not surrogate objective improvement alone. |

### Dynamics, statistical physics, information, and learning theory

| ID / field | Established understanding or design handle | Scope and missing transfer | Proposed decisive test |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaos / Dynamical systems and chaos | Attractors, local sensitivity, and stability analyze evolving systems; random recurrent-network dynamics is a distinct setting ([Åström and Murray](https://people.duke.edu/~hpgavin/SystemID/References/Astrom-Feedback-2006.pdf), [Sompolinsky, Crisanti, and Sommers](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10039285/)). | Instability of open-loop prediction does not by itself settle feedback controllability, and random-network ensemble results do not certify trained finite models ([Åström and Murray](https://people.duke.edu/~hpgavin/SystemID/References/Astrom-Feedback-2006.pdf)). | Compare forecast horizon, invariant statistics, and closed-loop regulation separately under measured disturbances. |
| reservoir / Reservoir computing | Fixed recurrent dynamics plus a learned readout provide a simple recurrent baseline and demonstrated chaotic forecasting ([Jaeger and Haas](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15064413/), [Pathak et al.](https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevLett.120.024102)). | A reservoir good at prediction need not expose semantic state or remain useful under intervention ([Pathak et al.](https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevLett.120.024102)). | Train only the readout, then compare with fully trained recurrence on unseen control inputs and correction cost. |
| lowrank / Low-rank RNN dynamics | Designed low-rank connectivity supports reduced dynamical descriptions linking connectivity and computation ([Mastrogiuseppe and Ostojic](https://arxiv.org/html/1711.09672v2)). | The reduction depends on structure and ensemble assumptions; a low-dimensional coordinate is not automatically a named semantic variable ([Mastrogiuseppe and Ostojic](https://arxiv.org/html/1711.09672v2)). | Construct task-bearing low-rank models and test whether the reduced dynamics predicts held-out intervention responses, not just ordinary trajectories. |
| dmft / Task-trained recurrent DMFT | Dynamical mean-field theory studies aggregate correlations and dynamics of large trained recurrent systems ([Clark, Bordelon, Zavatone-Veth, and Pehlevan](https://kempnerinstitute.harvard.edu/research/deeper-learning/structure-disorder-and-dynamics-in-task-trained-recurrent-neural-circuits/)). | Ensemble or large-width behavior is not a certificate for one finite model or a semantic control law ([Kempner research account](https://kempnerinstitute.harvard.edu/research/deeper-learning/structure-disorder-and-dynamics-in-task-trained-recurrent-neural-circuits/)). | Pre-register finite-width corrections and compare predicted response distributions with trained networks across seeds and task changes. |
| critical / Criticality and signal propagation | Initialization theory links propagation depth to correlations and gradients in specified wide-network regimes ([Schoenholz, Gilmer, Ganguli, and Sohl-Dickstein](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1611.01232v2)). | The analyzed critical regime is not a universal optimum for intelligence, safety, or correction reliability ([Schoenholz et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1611.01232v2)). | Match training compute across subcritical, near-critical, and supercritical initializations; compare learned utility and intervention robustness. |
| deeplinear / Deep linear learning dynamics | Exact reduced analyses show nonlinear, stagewise parameter learning even with a linear end-to-end map ([Saxe, McClelland, and Ganguli](https://www.saxelab.org/assets/papers/Saxe2013a.pdf)). | Solvable deep-linear dynamics do not establish analogous global laws for arbitrary nonlinear training ([Saxe et al.](https://www.saxelab.org/assets/papers/Saxe2013a.pdf)). | Test curriculum and data reweighting predictions first where the equations are exact, then quantify failures in nonlinear extensions. |
| ntk / Neural tangent kernels | In a specified infinite-width training regime, function evolution is described by a limiting kernel ([Jacot, Gabriel, and Hongler](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.07572)). | Fixed-kernel predictions need not capture substantial feature learning; the regime must be checked, not presumed ([Jacot et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.07572)). | Predict a small training intervention with a kernel model and compare with actual retraining across width and training duration. |
| info / Information theory | Entropy, mutual information, coding, and capacity quantify specified random variables and communication channels ([Shannon](https://people.math.harvard.edu/~ctm/home/text/others/shannon/entropy/entropy.pdf)). | Information quantity alone does not define which facts or goals matter; the variables and task determine its relevance ([Shannon](https://people.math.harvard.edu/~ctm/home/text/others/shannon/entropy/entropy.pdf), [Tishby, Pereira, and Bialek](https://www.princeton.edu/~wbialek/our_papers/tishby+al_99.pdf)). | Specify the target before measuring information; test whether retained bits predict action consequences and necessary safety distinctions. |
| ib / Information bottleneck | Optimize compression relative to a designated relevant variable ([Tishby, Pereira, and Bialek](https://www.princeton.edu/~wbialek/our_papers/tishby+al_99.pdf)). | A general compression-causes-generalization account is not supported across all networks; estimator and nonlinearity choices matter ([Saxe, Bansal, and collaborators](https://research.ibm.com/publications/on-the-information-bottleneck-theory-of-deep-learning)). | Change the task and constraint targets while holding compression level fixed; test which consequential distinctions disappear. |
| rate / Information-limited feedback | Unstable plant modes imply communication requirements for stabilization in a specified stochastic-control setting ([Nair and Evans](https://people.eng.unimelb.edu.au/gnair/NairSICON04.pdf)). | A data-rate theorem is not a universal hidden-state-size bound or an explanation of language intelligence ([Nair and Evans](https://people.eng.unimelb.edu.au/gnair/NairSICON04.pdf)). | Impose channel rate, latency, and packet-loss constraints on an actual loop and test the theorem’s assumptions explicitly. |
| slt / Singular learning theory | Bayesian evidence and generalization geometry can differ from regular parameter-count approximations ([Watanabe](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/volume14/watanabe13a/watanabe13a.pdf)). | Bayesian asymptotics under analytic/statistical conditions do not directly specify SGD trajectories, semantic objectives, or a controller ([Watanabe](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/volume14/watanabe13a/watanabe13a.pdf)). | Ask for one quantitative training-response prediction that beats a simpler baseline; do not treat the learning coefficient itself as safety. |
| llc / Local learning coefficient | A localized complexity estimator connects sampled loss geometry to a local learning coefficient ([Lau, Furman, Wang, Murfet, and Wei](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.12108)). | Localization, temperature, sampling, and local-minimum assumptions affect interpretation; estimates are not direct semantic labels ([Lau et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.12108)). | Establish estimator repeatability first, then test incremental predictive value for intervention outcomes beyond loss, gradients, and behavior. |
| development / Developmental learning theory | Training stages have been studied through loss geometry and behavioral changes; stagewise attribution analyzes changing training-data influence ([Hoogland and collaborators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02364), [Lee, Smith, Adam, and Hoogland](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.12071v1)). | Detecting a stage is not controlling it; the link from Bayesian analysis to non-equilibrium SGD remains a stated limitation ([Lee et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.12071v1)). | Select interventions using early checkpoints only; predict correction efficacy at later checkpoints and compare with ordinary validation-based selection. |
| mdl / MDL and singular compression | Description length and singular statistical complexity have a mathematical connection under specified assumptions, with reported compression correlations ([Urdshals, Lau, Hoogland, van Wingerden, and Murfet](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.12077)). | Compression is not semantic preservation, and correlation with compressibility does not establish a safe-control metric ([Urdshals et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.12077)). | Measure loss, capability, rare-constraint behavior, and correction retention before and after compression at matched deployment cost. |
| thesis / Hoogland’s master’s thesis | The verified title is The Ergodic Theory of Random Neural Networks; his biography lists a University of Amsterdam physics master’s in 2019–2021 and supervisor Greg Stephens ([Hoogland](https://www.jessehoogland.com/about)). | Full text was not located in the preceding investigation; no thesis-specific equations, guarantees, or findings are asserted. Ergodicity and controllability are not interchangeable definitions. | Obtain the thesis before tracing its actual results into training or control hypotheses. Use the independently sourced RNN and learning-theory literature meanwhile. |

### Expanded construction routes and capable systems

| ID / field | Established understanding or design handle | Scope and missing transfer | Proposed decisive test |
|---|---|---|---|
| mlp / Feed-forward and residual networks | Explicit function composition provides a general learned map; optimization layers can embed constrained subproblems within it ([Rumelhart, Hinton, and Williams](https://www.nature.com/articles/323533a0), [Amos and Kolter](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.00443)). | A differentiable computational graph is not a human semantic model; layer constraints cover their stated mathematical variables ([Amos and Kolter](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.00443)). | Compare unconstrained prediction with an embedded feasible-action layer and test solver tolerances, infeasibility, and capability cost. |
| reduction / Dimensionality reduction and reduced-order models | Low-dimensional representations can simplify learning or dynamics; singular-mode analysis gives a controlled example ([Saxe et al.](https://www.saxelab.org/assets/papers/Saxe2013a.pdf)). | Preserving high-variance directions is not the same objective as preserving controllable or safety-relevant distinctions; control uses input-output structure ([Åström and Murray](https://people.duke.edu/~hpgavin/SystemID/References/Astrom-Feedback-2006.pdf)). | Compare prediction-optimal compression with action- and constraint-aware compression on rare, consequential states. |
| hopfield / Energy-based associative dynamics | Symmetric associative-memory constructions expose an energy function with decreasing dynamics under the specified update rule ([Hopfield](https://redwood.berkeley.edu/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/hopfield82.pdf)). | Convergence to an attractor does not guarantee convergence to the intended memory; the energy argument does not cover every asymmetric recurrent network ([Hopfield](https://redwood.berkeley.edu/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/hopfield82.pdf)). | Corrupt inputs, add spurious memories, and test whether energy descent predicts successful retrieval or merely stable wrong answers. |
| gam / Additive, monotonic, and interpretable predictors | Interpretable-by-design modeling is an alternative to post-hoc explanations; deep lattice networks can guarantee selected monotonic relations ([Rudin](http://www.arxiv.org/abs/1811.10154), [You et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.06680)). | A monotonic relationship must be appropriate for the actual task; guaranteeing the wrong shape does not make the prediction correct ([You et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.06680)). | Compare compact additive/monotonic models with flexible baselines; test whether domain experts’ stated constraints hold under interventions. |
| concept / Concept bottlenecks | Supervised concepts become explicit editable intermediate predictions ([Koh et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/koh20a/koh20a.pdf)). | Model-level concept edits are not automatically interventions on reality; even concept-predictive channels can retain nuisance information ([Koh et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/koh20a/koh20a.pdf), [Almudévar, Hernández-Lobato, and Ortega, 2026 version](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.04877v3)). | Test edit accuracy, side effects, nuisance leakage, and performance after replacing all concepts with verified values. |
| causal / Causal representation learning | Appropriate intervention data and structural assumptions can reduce latent ambiguity; some results identify factors up to permutation, shift, and scaling ([Ahuja, Mahajan, Wang, and Bengio](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/ahuja23a/ahuja23a.pdf)). | Theorems use specified decoder/support/intervention conditions; arbitrary pixels, language, and real interventions do not automatically satisfy them ([Ahuja et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/ahuja23a/ahuja23a.pdf)). | Use known intervention targets during training but hold out combinations and mechanisms; quantify ambiguity instead of requiring arbitrary coordinate agreement. |
| ren / Contracting recurrent equilibrium networks | Parameterizations can enforce well-posedness, contraction, and selected incremental gain/passivity constraints during learning ([Revay, Wang, and Manchester](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.05942)). | State and input-output robustness are not semantic correctness; persistent-memory capability can conflict with uniform contraction ([Revay et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.05942), [Miller and Hardt](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.10369)). | Learn real task dynamics at increasing complexity; compare robust utility and delayed memory against an equally tuned unconstrained model. |
| port / Port-Hamiltonian and dissipative neural control | Energy storage, dissipation, and input-output ports support compositional control design ([Zakwan and Ferrari-Trecate](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2403.17785)). | Closed-loop guarantees need compatible plant/interconnection assumptions; continuous-time gain does not automatically survive digital implementation ([Zakwan and Ferrari-Trecate](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2403.17785)). | Test the discretized controller, sensor delays, contact, and non-passive components; verify both energy accounting and useful task completion. |
| equivariant / Equivariant architectures | Specified transformation laws can hold by construction when all components respect the group action ([Kondor](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12541325/)). | Equivariance means the answer transforms consistently, not that it is accurate or that every task shares the symmetry ([Kondor](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12541325/)). | Rotate, translate, or permute the world and desired output together; separately test symmetry-breaking context such as gravity or fixed obstacles. |
| constrainedrl / Constrained RL, shields, and safety filters | Expected-cost optimization, temporal-logic shields, and predictive safety filters provide different constraint mechanisms ([Achiam et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/achiam17a/achiam17a.pdf), [Alshiekh et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.08611), [Wabersich and Zeilinger](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1812.05506)). | Expected discounted cost is not every-trajectory safety; filtering needs confidence bounds, feasibility, and a safe fallback ([Achiam et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/achiam17a/achiam17a.pdf), [Wabersich and Zeilinger](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1812.05506)). | Measure unsafe-success episodes and severity, plus utility lost through intervention; deliberately test model-confidence failure and fallback feasibility. |
| preference / Preference learning, RLHF, DPO, and assistance | Preference data shapes behavior; DPO directly fits preference pairs, while CIRL models uncertainty about a human reward ([Christiano et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03741), [Rafailov et al.](https://papers.baulab.info/papers/also/Rafailov-2023.pdf), [Hadfield-Menell et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.03137)). | Preferences and reward models are proxies; overoptimization and reward non-uniqueness obstruct an automatic inference of actual intent ([Gao, Schulman, and Hilton](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.10760), [Rafailov et al.](https://papers.baulab.info/papers/also/Rafailov-2023.pdf)). | Hold out evaluators and preference contexts; test explicit corrections, ambiguity questions, proxy exploitation, and preserved capability. |
| llm / LLMs and reasoning models | Pretraining, instruction training, process feedback, verifiable-outcome RL, and compositional curricula provide different capability-building levers ([Ouyang et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02155), [Lightman et al.](http://arxiv.org/abs/2305.20050), [Guo et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12443585/), [Lake and Baroni](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37880371/)). | Benchmark success, preference gains, and readable reasoning do not establish universal semantic control; even measured prompt controllability is task- and distribution-dependent ([Cheng et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.05637v1)). | Train a model to learn unfamiliar rule systems; test held-out compositional tasks, verifier-correct solutions, conflicts, clarification, and persistent corrections at matched compute. |
| vlm / Vision-language models | Contrastive image-text training and visual instruction tuning provide language-addressable visual behavior ([Radford et al.](https://cdn.openai.com/papers/Learning_Transferable_Visual_Models_From_Natural_Language.pdf), [Liu, Li, Wu, and Lee](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.08485)). | Text-image similarity or an articulate answer does not establish causal scene understanding or correct grounding ([Radford et al.](https://cdn.openai.com/papers/Learning_Transferable_Visual_Models_From_Natural_Language.pdf)). | Separate perception, relational reasoning, instruction priority, and uncertainty tests; counterbalance text priors against visual evidence. |
| vla / Vision-language-action models | RT-2 uses action tokens, OpenVLA fine-tunes a VLM for actions, and π₀ generates continuous action chunks through flow matching ([Brohan et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2307.15818v1), [Kim et al.](https://github.com/openvla/openvla), [Black et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.24164)). | Valid action tokens are not valid trajectories; physical skills and safety remain separate from transferred web semantics ([RT-2](https://arxiv.org/html/2307.15818v1), [Fan et al., SafeVLA-Bench](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.00773v3)). | Jointly score task success, instruction correctness, whole-trajectory safety, interruptions, and new embodiment transfer. |
| videoworld / Video and latent predictive world models | V-JEPA 2 separates action-free visual pretraining from action-conditioned robotic post-training ([Assran et al.](https://ai.meta.com/research/publications/v-jepa-2-self-supervised-video-models-enable-understanding-prediction-and-planning/)). | Video plausibility and scene metrics need not translate into reliable closed-loop action consequences ([Liang et al., WorldLens](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/papers/Liang_WorldLens_Full-Spectrum_Evaluations_of_Driving_World_Models_in_Real_World_CVPR_2026_paper.pdf)). | Intervene on actions, camera visibility, object identity, and hidden state; compare predicted consequences to actual held-out rollouts. |
| objectworld / Object-centric and relational world models | Slot Attention creates exchangeable object-oriented slots; graph models provide relational structure ([Locatello et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.15055), [Battaglia et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.01261)). | A slot is not guaranteed to be a persistent physical object or a causally identified variable ([Locatello et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.15055), [Ahuja et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/ahuja23a/ahuja23a.pdf)). | Occlude and re-identify objects; change their properties and interactions; test object count, persistence, and controlled relation changes. |
| neurosymbolic / Program learning and neural-symbolic reasoning | DreamCoder learns reusable program libraries; AlphaGeometry combines a learned proposer with symbolic deduction ([Ellis and collaborators](https://royalsocietypublishing.org/doi/10.1098/rsta.2022.0050), [Trinh, Wu, Le, He, and Luong](https://research.google/pubs/solving-olympiad-geometry-without-human-demonstrations/)). | Executable structure makes some computations inspectable, but specification coverage, search cost, and translation into the formal domain remain limits ([DreamCoder](https://royalsocietypublishing.org/doi/10.1098/rsta.2022.0050), [AlphaGeometry](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06747-5)). | Learn reusable rules on unfamiliar tasks, then test novel compositions, deliberately wrong formalizations, and proof/checker failures. |

## What the new 2026 evidence changes

- **Generative controllability is measurable, but scoped.** Emily Cheng and collaborators’ GenCtrl estimates reachable and controllable output-attribute sets under specified prompt/intervention distributions, quantization, and horizons; its finite-sample guarantees are about those estimates, not arbitrary semantic compliance ([full January manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.05637v1), [Apple’s ICLR publication listing](https://machinelearning.apple.com/research/genctrl)). This is directly relevant without reverse engineering: it asks whether the desired outcomes are actually reachable through the allowed interface.
- **Safety and success can disagree substantially.** Fan and collaborators’ October 2, 2026 SafeVLA-Bench preprint reports that its fifteen LIBERO policies exceeding 90% mean task success still have 18–28% unsafe-episode rates under the benchmark’s specified clauses ([full preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.00773v3)). These are simulator-based, threshold-dependent findings, not estimates of real-world accident rates.
- **A concrete VLA/control bridge exists, with a visible gap.** Hu and collaborators’ July 2026 AEGIS version combines VLM obstacle selection with a CBF action layer, but its collision theorem assumes accurate assessment, adequate point-cloud filtering, complete obstacle representation, safe initialization, and exact enforcement; its guarantee covers the modeled end-effector rather than every robot link ([VLSA v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.11891v2)). This makes perception-to-constraint validity a load-bearing part of the claim, not a detail.
- **Verification is useful but not frontier-model certification.** Sasaki and collaborators’ ARCH-COMP26 evaluates specified neural-controller/plant systems over bounded properties and reports verified, falsified, and unknown outcomes; the benchmark networks are much smaller than frontier language models, and even sample-and-hold versus continuous feedback can change the result ([competition report](https://mediatum.ub.tum.de/doc/1858071/o9dt7fraj5g7m3rsokp3o0zca.ARCH26_pages_85-130.pdf)). A verification result must travel with its modeled dynamics, initial set, numerical treatment, and horizon.

## Research paths with the clearest constructive promise

The following are recommendations from this synthesis, not a ranking demonstrated by one benchmark. Each combines a capability mechanism with an independently testable control claim.

| Path | Capability-bearing construction | What to establish before scaling |
|---|---|---|
| Learned scientific controller | Identified state + structured/residual dynamics + uncertainty-aware MPC | Action-conditioned validity, safe exploration, recursive feasibility, improved task utility over classical baselines |
| Correctable recurrent learner | Persistent explicit memory + adaptable recurrent state + trained correction interface | Corrections survive distractors and time without unacceptable capability loss or unintended behavior changes |
| Grounded multimodal world model | Object/relational state + action-conditioned prediction + language specification interface | State persistence, uncertainty, counterfactual accuracy, valid translation of language into goals and constraints |
| Verifiable reasoning learner | Learned proposer + executable hypotheses/programs + domain checker | Generalization to new rules and compositions, checker soundness, useful performance within search budget |
| Intervention-aware foundation model | Ordinary scalable backbone + curated curricula + explicit edit/supervision interfaces | Predictable behavioral response to training and user corrections, evaluator-independent transfer, robust capability |

No backbone wins these paths by name alone. The companion construction document defines an evaluation program that tests learned capability and controllability together, while the mathematical document states where equivalences and guarantees stop.
